# Archive

Superseded working material, kept for provenance. Nothing here is cited by the
manuscript (`docs/final_paper_v27.md`) and nothing here should be read as a
current claim.

- `paper_draft.md` — an earlier and longer measurement-study write-up of the
  same experimental data: overhead magnitudes, the strategy ranking, the
  eleven-run replication and the detectable-effect analysis. Its content is
  folded into `docs/final_paper_v27.md`, which is the only manuscript derived
  from this dataset. This draft was never submitted to any venue and is not a
  companion or related publication.

- `figures/`, `generate_paper_figures.py` — figures belonging to
  `paper_draft.md` and the script that produces them. The manuscript's own
  figures are `docs/figures/axes_fig*`, produced by
  `scripts/generate_design_axes_figures.py`.
